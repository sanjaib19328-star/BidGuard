ANNEXURE-2: INSTRUCTIONS TO THE BIDDERS
1. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONS
1.1 Office of the Comptroller and Auditor General of India (the "Client") invites e-bids under Two Bid System (Technical and
Financial) through GeM from reputed firms dealing with Ministries/Govt. departments for providing hired manpower
services.
1.3 The Bidder should download the Bid document through GeM Portal and upload it again with signature and stamp,
acknowledging acceptance of the entire Bid document.
1.5 Conditional offers, offers with deviations, bids not meeting minimum eligibility criteria, or Technical Bids not
accompanied with EMD of requisite amount/format are liable to be rejected.

2. MINIMUM ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
a. Legal Valid Entity: The Bidder shall necessarily be a legally valid entity, either a Limited Company / Private Limited
Company registered under the Companies Act, 2013, or an LLP under the LLP Act, 2008. Bidder in the form of JV/Consortium,
Proprietorship, and Partnership is NOT permitted. A proof for supporting the legal validity of the Bidder shall be submitted.
b. Registration: The Bidder should be registered with the Income Tax, Goods and Service Tax and also registered under the
labour laws, Employees Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO), Employees State Insurance Corporation (ESIC).
c. Clearance: The Bidder should also have clearance from GST, and Income Tax Department. Relevant proof in support shall
be submitted.
d. Experience: The Bidder should have (i) at least three years' experience for providing manpower services in Ministries /
Departments under Government of India and (ii) experience in providing manpower services in any of the sensitive / VVIP
areas like PMO, IB, Ministries /Departments of GOI, President's Secretariat, Parliament, State Assemblies, Supreme Court of
India and High Court.
e. Turnover: The Bidder should have minimum gross turnover in the similar business of providing manpower as per the bid
document.
f. The bidder should have its own trained manpower on their rolls. A Notarized affidavit and undertaking that the workers
employed would be paid at least minimum wages (both for skilled and unskilled) as per orders of Govt. of NCT of Delhi and
oblige all statutory requirements with respect to ESI, EPF etc.
g. The bidder should have its office located in Delhi / NCT of Delhi region. The Bidder should submit documentary proof for
the same.

2.1 Documents supporting the Minimum Eligibility Criteria
(i) 2(a): attested copy of Incorporation Certificate issued by the Companies Registrar/concerned authority.
(ii) 2(b): attested copy of PAN, GST Registration, EPF Registration, ESIC Registration and Labour Licence.
(iii) 2(c): attested copy of ITR and GST/Service Tax Return for the last three financial years.
(iv) 2(d): attested copy of work orders alongwith work completion certificates / ongoing work certificates + notarized
affidavit per Annexure-9.
(v) 2(e): attested copy of the audited balance sheets (audited by Chartered Accountant) for the completed three financial
years i.e. 2017-18, 2018-19 and 2019-20.
(vi) Other documents as per Clause 6 of Annexure-2.
